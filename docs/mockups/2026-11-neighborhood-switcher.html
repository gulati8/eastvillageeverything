Neighborhood switcher — three layouts

Tap the arrow beside the title to open the neighborhood row, tap a pill to switch and close it; the list is fetched once and filtered on the device, and saved places ignore the switch.

The masthead is the same on every phone: the brand and the clock in the eyebrow, the neighborhood as the title. The columns differ only in where the search box and the filter button sit. Each column shows the row collapsed, then expanded. Neighborhood names, order and counts are today's prod data, as are the places. Pick layout 1, 2 or 3 in task 4.2.

Layout 1 — search row under the title
Search and filter stay where they are today: one row under the title. The neighborhood row opens between the title and that row.
