Neighborhood switcher — three options

The app is about to cover more than the East Village. Each column is one way to let a reader pick a neighborhood on the list screen, drawn in the app's dark palette (darkColors in apps/mobile/src/theme/tokens.ts) with the three Google Fonts the app loads (Instrument Serif, Source Serif 4, Schibsted Grotesk). The top phone is the closed state; the bottom phone is what a tap produces. Place names, streets and italic lines are real rows from prod; the neighborhood counts are from the addresses in today's data and are rough until 4.5 assigns rows.

The brand stays East Village Everything in every option. Pick A, B or C in task 4.2.

How it works underneath, in every option: the app fetches the whole place list once and filters it on the device, so switching is instant and works offline. The pick is remembered on the device. Saved places ignore the switch: the Saved tab always shows everything you bookmarked, from any neighborhood.
Option A — the masthead is the neighborhood
The eyebrow carries the brand on the left and the clock on the right. The big italic title becomes the neighborhood name with a chevron; tapping it drops a menu under the title.
