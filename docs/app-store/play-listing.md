# Google Play listing: East Village Everything

Source of truth for the Play Console listing of the Android app in
`apps/mobile/` (package `com.eastvillageeverything.app`, version 1.0.0,
`versionCode` 1). `node scripts/app-store-metadata.mjs` generates the fastlane
`supply` tree `apps/mobile/fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/` from this file:
the fenced blocks under Name, Short description and Full description become
`title.txt`, `short_description.txt` and `full_description.txt`, and the
graphics under `docs/app-store/android/` are copied into `images/`. Keep the
shape of the fenced blocks. The Data safety, content rating and App content
answers below are entered in Play Console by hand (task 2.7); `supply` cannot
set them.

The iOS listing is `listing.md`. The two descriptions say the same things in
the same voice; the only Android differences are "Maps" for "Apple Maps" and
"phone" for "iPhone". When a feature changes, update both files in the same
commit.

Character limits are Play Console's. Counts are given so a copy change can be
checked without pasting into the console (`wc -c` on the generated files
includes a trailing newline).

## Full description

4000 character limit. Blank lines are paragraph breaks; Play keeps them.
Play Console does not render Markdown, and the uppercase section headings are
plain text.

```
East Village Everything is a guide to the bars and late-night spots of the East Village, Manhattan. Nearly 250 places in one list, with the details that matter when you're already out: where the happy hour is still going, who has a pool table or a dart board, where there's trivia tonight, and what's open late.

BROWSE AND SEARCH
Scroll the whole neighborhood or search by name. Each place shows its address, hours, phone number, website and tags, plus the current specials and our notes on what it's good for.

FILTER BY WHAT YOU'RE IN THE MOOD FOR
Tap tags to narrow the list: Happy Hours, Late Night Eats, Live Music, Trivia Night, Pool Tables, Darts, Skeeball, Ping Pong, Karaoke, Dancing, Sports Bars, Wine Bar, BYOB, Brunch and more. Combine them. Sort A to Z or by what's closing soonest.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
Places with hours show whether they're open now and when they close. Rows call out live music tonight, late closes and happy hours so you can pick at a glance.

SAVE YOUR SPOTS
Tap the bookmark on any place to keep it in your Saved list. Saved places stay on your phone and survive relaunches.

WORKS WITHOUT A SIGNAL
The list is cached on your device, so it opens instantly and still works in a basement bar with no reception. Pull down to refresh when you're back online.

ONE TAP TO CALL, VISIT OR GET THERE
Phone numbers dial. Websites open in your browser. Tap the address for directions in Maps.

NO ACCOUNT, NO TRACKING
There is nothing to sign up for. The app does not ask for your location, does not show ads and does not use third-party analytics. Details in the privacy policy at https://eastvillageeverything.nyc/privacy.

East Village Everything is made in the East Village and covers the East Village only. If your favorite spot is missing or something is wrong, tell us at https://eastvillageeverything.nyc/support.
```

About 1,900 of 4000 characters. The facts it relies on are listed under
"## Description" in `listing.md`; the Android build is the same JavaScript,
so they hold here too. On Android the address opens a `geo:` search, which
lands in whatever maps app the phone has (Google Maps on most), hence "Maps".

## App content: Data safety

Policy > App content > Data safety. Answers for every question in the form.

### Overview

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | Yes |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | Yes (the API and Sentry are HTTPS only) |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | No. The device ID lives only on the phone and is gone when the app is uninstalled; nothing is linked to a person, so there is nothing to look up and delete. |

### Data types collected

Exactly two. Everything else is "not collected".

| Data type | Group | Collected | Shared | Ephemeral | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Device or other IDs | Device or other IDs | Yes | No | No | Required (no opt-out) | App functionality |
| Crash logs | App info and performance | Yes | No | No | Required (no opt-out) | App functionality |
| Diagnostics | App info and performance | Yes | No | No | Required (no opt-out) | App functionality |

Why:

- Device or other IDs: on first launch the app generates a random UUID,
  stores it in the Android Keystore-backed SecureStore and sends it as
  `X-Device-Id` on every API request (`src/identity/deviceId.ts`). It is
  used for rate limiting and debugging. There are no accounts, so it is not
  linked to a person. It is scrubbed from Sentry breadcrumbs
  (`src/observability/sentry.ts`).
- Crash logs and Diagnostics: Sentry (`@sentry/react-native`) receives crash
  reports from production builds with the device model, OS version and app
  version, and performance traces at a 10 percent sample rate
  (`tracesSampleRate: 0.1`). Sentry processes this on our behalf under its
  terms as a service provider, which Google's form does not count as
  "sharing".

### Not collected

Personal info (name, email, user IDs, address, phone, other), financial info,
health and fitness, messages, photos and videos, audio, files and docs,
calendar, contacts, app activity (interactions, search history, installed
apps, other user-generated content, other actions), web browsing, and
location (approximate and precise). Saved places and the list cache live only
in AsyncStorage on the device and never leave it. The app declares no
Android permissions beyond `INTERNET`.
